E2. Superimposing the Ras domains of the nucleotide-bound Gs-alpha structure and of the complex leaves no overlap between Nb35 and the alpha-helical domain.

E4. Double electron-electron resonance on a related G protein bound to light-activated rhodopsin measures distance changes of up to 20 A between the two domains.
E5. Apyrase was used to destroy the GDP released on complex formation, since GDP or GTP analogues disrupt the high-affinity complex; deuterium exchange at the interface between the two domains increases on complex formation.
