The crystal structure of the beta2 adrenergic receptor bound to an agonist and to the nucleotide-free Gs heterotrimer (3.2 A) shows the alpha-helical domain of Gs-alpha swung away from its Ras-like GTPase domain, by about 127 degrees. A nanobody, Nb35, had been added to make the complex crystallize. The attached evidence.txt gives the evidence (facts E1–E5 from Rasmussen et al., Nature 477, 549 (2011); numbers as reported in the paper). Which account of the displaced alpha-helical domain is consistent with all of these observations?

选项：
A. GDP is still bound in a closed nucleotide pocket, and the apparent displacement is a modelling error.
B. Nb35 pushed the alpha-helical domain aside, so the displacement is an artefact of the crystallization aid.
C. The two domains stay together in solution; only the crystal lattice pulls them apart.
D. Removing the nucleotide unlocks the two domains, so the alpha-helical domain becomes mobile; the position seen in the crystal is only one of the conformations it can adopt, selected by crystal packing.

E1. Nb35 binds at the interface between the Gs-beta subunit and the Ras-like domain of Gs-alpha; none of its contacts on the Ras domain are residues that touch the alpha-helical domain in the nucleotide-bound structure.

E5. Apyrase was used to destroy the GDP released on complex formation, since GDP or GTP analogues disrupt the high-affinity complex; deuterium exchange at the interface between the two domains increases on complex formation.
