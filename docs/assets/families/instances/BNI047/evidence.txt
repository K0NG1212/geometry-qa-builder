E1. Both mutants are trapped with the same activation gate opening of 22 A (measured between Thr112 alpha-carbons on diagonal subunits).
E2. In single-channel recordings the E71A construct has a high open probability (0.8 to 0.9) and the Y82A construct a low one (0.015 to 0.04), both independent of pH.
E3. In the E71A structure (2.25 A) the selectivity filter holds four potassium ions in the usual 1,3 and 2,4 arrangement, and its backbone differs from the closed conductive state by only 0.19 to 0.26 A r.m.s.d.
E4. In the Y82A structure (2.4 A) the filter has lost the ions at sites 2 and 3, the Val76 carbonyl has flipped out of the axis, and the inter-subunit distance at Gly77 is reduced from 8 to 6 A.
E5. In the inactivated structure three ordered water molecules sit in a cavity behind the filter and hydrogen bond to the flipped Val76 carbonyl; they are absent from the conductive filter.
