BU1-CHCl3: BU1 in chloroform — Six (S)-1-phenylethyl groups and six methyl groups on the portals, the phenylethyl groups at the R1 positions. These bulkier substituents (no CH2 spacer; the phenyl sits on a stereocentre carrying a methyl group) take up the closed arrangement over the cavity axis and twist the cavity into a screw: the two methine C-H...X- contacts of each glycoluril unit differ in length by about 0.3 A. Titrated in chloroform.
BU1-MeCN: BU1 in acetonitrile — BU1 as described for the chloroform candidate, with the same portal substituents and the same screw-distorted cavity, but titrated in acetonitrile instead of chloroform; the macrocycle is unchanged.
BU2-CHCl3: BU2 in chloroform — The diastereomer of BU1, the same six (S)-1-phenylethyl and six methyl groups but exchanged between the R1 and R2 positions of each glycoluril. The phenylethyl groups point away from the cavity (the open arrangement) and all twelve C-H...X- contacts come out equal in length, so the rigidified cavity is undistorted. Titrated in chloroform.
BnBU-CHCl3: BnBU in chloroform — Twelve benzyl groups on the portals. A CH2 spacer separates each portal nitrogen from its phenyl ring, so the rings can swing clear of the cavity axis and the macrocycle stays flexible and adapts to the anion; in its chloride complex all twelve C-H...Cl- contacts are equal at 3.13 A. Titrated in chloroform.
