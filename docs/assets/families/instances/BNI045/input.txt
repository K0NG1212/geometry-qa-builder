Anthrax protective antigen (PA) heptamers convert at endosomal low pH from a soluble prepore into a membrane-spanning pore. Cryo-EM maps of the pore are compared with crystal structures of prepores. The attached evidence.txt gives the evidence (facts E1–E5 from Jiang et al., Nature 521, 545 (2015); numbers as reported in the paper). Which account of the low-pH conversion is consistent with all of these observations?

选项：
A. The beta barrel forms first and pulls the domains together, which then closes the phi clamp.
B. The 2beta10-2beta11 loop acts as the pH sensor and flips first; the domains then converge and assemble the phi clamp, and the beta barrel forms afterwards as a consequence, not as the cause.
C. Protonation of histidine residues in the sensing loop triggers the conversion.
D. The domain 2 / domain 4 interface is the pH sensor: breaking it is what makes the structure respond to acid.

===== evidence.txt =====
E1. A subset of 21,632 particles gives a 3.6 A map that is in the pore state, with the domains 2c converged and the phi clamp correctly assembled, but without the 14-stranded beta barrel.
E2. The 2beta10-2beta11 loop is in the pore conformation in one octameric prepore crystal structure and in the prepore conformation in another, while the rest of the two structures is largely unchanged.
E3. Mutating the conserved Asn422 or Asp425 in that loop abolishes the low-pH conversion from prepore to pore.
E4. The loop contains no histidine, the residue whose pKa of about 6 falls in the pH range where the conversion happens.
E5. Mutations near the domain 2 / domain 4 interface, or deletion of the 2beta2-2beta3 loop, shift the pH threshold of the conversion but do not abolish its pH sensitivity.
