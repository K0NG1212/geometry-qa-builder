E1. Band structures computed with and without the cation agree near the gap; the cation states lie several eV away from the band edges.
E2. In the measured structures of methylammonium lead iodide the Pb-I bond lengths deviate by at most 0.1 per cent from their average of 3.18 A, and the diagonals of each octahedron are within 4 degrees of perpendicular.
E3. A model built from perfectly regular corner-sharing octahedra with no cation at all reproduces a gap that varies by up to about 1 eV with the Pb-I-Pb bond angles, following the largest of those angles.
E4. At fixed bond angles, the spread of Pb-I bond lengths among the realistic structures changes the gap by about 0.2 eV (roughly 3 eV per angstrom).
