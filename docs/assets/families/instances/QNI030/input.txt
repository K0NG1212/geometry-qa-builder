STM topographs of twisted bilayer graphene near the magic angle give the moiré wavelength along the three principal directions of the moiré lattice. The graphene lattice constant is a = 0.246 nm; an unstrained twist θ gives a single moiré period λ = a / (2 sin(θ/2)). The attached evidence.txt gives the evidence (facts E1–E3 from Kerelsky et al., Nature 572, 95 (2019); numbers as reported in the paper). A strain-unaware analysis treats the triangle formed by the three wavelengths as an equilateral triangle of the same area. Find the twist angle this gives and decide what the unequal wavelengths mean. (a = 0.246 nm.) Choose the option in which both the value and the conclusion are correct.

选项：
