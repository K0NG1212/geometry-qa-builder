E1. The separation between the two vHs peaks falls steadily as the twist angle decreases: it is still about 55 meV at 1.10°, and the peaks have nearly merged (13 meV) at 0.79°.
E2. The half-width of each vHs peak is smallest near 1.10°; at 0.79° the peaks are closer together but wider.
E3. Superconductivity and correlated insulators are seen in transport at about 1.1°.
E4. A second near-magic-angle sample with a different heterostrain (0.6 %) shows a similar vHs splitting, and a tight-binding model with the experimental Fermi velocity reproduces the measured splittings.
