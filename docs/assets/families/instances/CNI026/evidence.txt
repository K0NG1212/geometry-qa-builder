E1. The LRCD coating (5.6 wt% dopant) reflects left-handed circularly polarised light in a band at 670 nm.
E2. The RRCD coating (4.7 wt% dopant) reflects right-handed circularly polarised light in a band at 603 nm.
E3. LRCD and RRCD give identical 1H and 13C NMR spectra and the same mass ([M+Na]+ at m/z 957.2).
E4. Optical rotation gives LRCD an enantiomeric excess of about 81 % relative to RRCD; the L-sorbitol starting material had an enantiomeric excess of about 76 ± 16 % relative to D-sorbitol.
