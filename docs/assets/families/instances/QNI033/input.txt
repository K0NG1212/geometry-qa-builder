===== evidence.txt =====
E1. Dark bands cross the images; inside them the vortex contrast is too weak to locate vortices.
E2. Tilting the specimen slightly, or changing the field (which changes the imaging condition), changes the size and position of the dark bands.
E3. The average vortex spacings measured in regions separated by the bands cross as the field rises, showing that vortices move from one region to another.
E4. Between the bands the vortex arrangement becomes more triangular as the field rises (average six-fold order parameter 0.73 at 117.3 G).
