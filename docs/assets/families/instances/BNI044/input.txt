The cryo-EM structure of the anthrax protective antigen (PA) pore at 2.9 A resolution shows the channel through which the enzymes lethal factor and edema factor cross the endosomal membrane. The attached evidence.txt gives the evidence (facts E1–E4 from Jiang et al., Nature 521, 545 (2015); numbers as reported in the paper). Which description of how a substrate protein passes through this channel is consistent with all of these facts?

选项：
A. The substrate has to be fully unfolded to pass the phi clamp, and may form an alpha helix again in the wider throat and tube below it.
B. Lethal factor passes through as an intact folded domain.
C. The substrate passes the phi clamp as preformed alpha helices.
D. The substrate has to stay fully extended along the whole channel, because no part of it is wide enough for a helix.

===== evidence.txt =====
E1. The mouth of the channel is 30 A wide at its opening and narrows to about 20 A inside; it can take secondary-structure elements but not folded domains such as the N-terminal domain of lethal factor.
E2. Below the mouth, a ring of seven Phe427 side chains (the phi clamp) leaves a solvent-excluded hole of only 6 A, the narrowest point of the whole channel.
E3. Below the clamp, the throat (about 18 A) and the tube formed by the 14-stranded beta barrel (inner diameter 12 to 18 A) are wide enough to hold an alpha helix.
E4. Apart from the mouth and the lower end of the tube the channel wall has no openings, and mutating even one of the seven Phe427 residues severely reduces translocation.
