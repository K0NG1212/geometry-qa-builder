Shubnikov-de Haas oscillations of a magic-angle twisted bilayer graphene (MA-TBG) device are mapped against carrier density n and magnetic field on the hole side; ns is the density that fills one superlattice band (4 electrons per moiré cell). The attached evidence.txt gives the evidence (facts E1–E5 from Cao et al., Nature 556, 43 (2018); numbers as reported in the paper). Which explanation of the extra Landau fan is consistent with all of these observations?

选项：
A. A doubled unit cell from strain or a charge density wave, which halves the filling factors of the ordinary single-particle bands.
B. Small Fermi pockets of quasiparticles counted from the half-filling correlated insulator, with half the usual degeneracy; the carriers above the superconducting dome are only |n| - ns/2.
C. Fractional Landau fans of the Hofstadter butterfly of the moiré superlattice.
D. The ordinary single-particle Fermi surface of all |n| carriers, with the usual four-fold degeneracy.

附件：evidence.txt（均为本题完整输入）。范围：证据取自论文，数值与观测按原文转述（不含原文段落）；只依据所给证据与通用科学知识作答，不需要也不允许查阅论文。 只回答一个字母 A、B、C 或 D。

===== evidence.txt =====
E1. The Landau fans that start at charge neutrality and at -ns have filling factors -4, -8, -12, ..., as expected from the four-fold spin and valley degeneracy of the single-particle bands.
E2. An extra fan starts at the half-filling insulating state -ns/2, with filling factors -2, -4, -6, ...; it sits directly above the superconducting dome.
E3. This extra fan is already visible at about 1 T.
E4. From the oscillation frequency, the number of degenerate Fermi pockets is M = 4 near charge neutrality and beyond the superlattice gap, but M = 2 for the oscillations that start near the half-filling state; their area follows |n| - ns/2 rather than |n|.
E5. The same Landau-level sequence is seen in two other MA-TBG devices.
