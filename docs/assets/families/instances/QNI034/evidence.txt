E1. The Landau fans that start at charge neutrality and at -ns have filling factors -4, -8, -12, ..., as expected from the four-fold spin and valley degeneracy of the single-particle bands.
E2. An extra fan starts at the half-filling insulating state -ns/2, with filling factors -2, -4, -6, ...; it sits directly above the superconducting dome.
E3. This extra fan is already visible at about 1 T.
E4. From the oscillation frequency, the number of degenerate Fermi pockets is M = 4 near charge neutrality and beyond the superlattice gap, but M = 2 for the oscillations that start near the half-filling state; their area follows |n| - ns/2 rather than |n|.
E5. The same Landau-level sequence is seen in two other MA-TBG devices.
