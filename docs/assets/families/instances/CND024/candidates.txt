C3SO3-: propanesulfonate, a three-carbon chain ending in a sulfonate group — The shortest chain of the four: with only three carbons there is almost nothing to bury in the hydrophobic channel. Its titration had to be run at a higher receptor concentration than the other three, which the reviewer-side notes record.
C4SO3-: butanesulfonate, a four-carbon chain ending in a sulfonate group — Half the chain length of the longest candidate, with the same charged head; only a short stretch of alkyl chain can enter the channel.
C6SO3-: hexanesulfonate, a six-carbon chain ending in a sulfonate group — Two carbons shorter than the longest candidate; the same sulfonate head sits at the same cationic rim, so two fewer CH2 groups are available to fill the hydrophobic channel.
C8SO3-: octanesulfonate, an eight-carbon chain ending in a sulfonate group — The longest chain of the four, so the greatest length of alkyl chain can be buried in the hydrophobic channel of the barrel while the sulfonate head stays at the cationic rim.
