A cationic pillar[5]arene is a barrel-shaped macrocycle of five hydroquinone units: the inside of the barrel is a hydrophobic aromatic channel, and each rim carries five positive charges. The guests below are straight-chain alkylsulfonates, so every one of them presents the same singly charged sulfonate head and differs only in how many carbons follow it. The paper reports that the alkyl chain is included in the hydrophobic aromatic cavity while the sulfonate group is held at the multicationic rim by ionic interactions, so the electrostatic part of the binding is the same for all four candidates and only the length of chain that can be buried inside the barrel changes. Association constants were measured in water by NMR and isothermal titration calorimetry. You need the guest this receptor holds most strongly, above three times ten million per mole. The attached candidates.txt describes four candidate structures, all of them prepared and measured in Gomez-Gonzalez et al., Pharmaceutics (2021). The candidate must satisfy: (affinity) the measured association constant in water is at least 3 × 10^7 M^-1. Exactly one of the four does so in the measured data, which is not shown to you.
