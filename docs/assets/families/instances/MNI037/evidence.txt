E1. On annealing, CsPbI3 films turn into a dark phase with a band gap of 1.73 eV, identified as the three-dimensional perovskite.
E2. The absorption edge moves continuously from 720 nm at x = 0 to 690 nm at x = 0.2.
E3. In the structures optimized by density functional theory, the Pb-I-Pb bond angles approach 180 degrees as the steric radius of the A-site cation grows (about 18 degrees per angstrom), and Rb+ is smaller than Cs+.
E4. The electronic states of the A-site cation lie several eV away from the band edges.
