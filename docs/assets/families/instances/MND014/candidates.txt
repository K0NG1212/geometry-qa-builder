Ag-Bpz: Ag-Bpz, a silver bipyrazolate framework — Table 1 gives a pore size of 3.5-8 A: Zeo++ puts the aperture at 3.5 A but finds the pore volume dominated by supermicropores at 3.5 and 8 A, and the framework is highly interpenetrated. Zeo++ also finds open metal sites here, at a density of 7.9 mmol cm^-3.
Cu-4Py-Me: Cu-4Py-Me, the copper triazolyl-isophthalate [Cu(Me-4py-trz-ia)] — Table 1 gives a single pore size of 5.5 A, the widest aperture of the four. Zeo++ classes it as ultramicroporous only, and finds no open metal sites.
Cu-tetrazolate: Cu-tetrazolate, a copper tetrazolate framework — Table 1 gives a pore size of 3.2-3.6 A, the tightest of the four; the short rigid tetrazolate linker is what generates so narrow an aperture. Zeo++ classes it as ultramicroporous only, and finds no open metal sites.
UTSA-16(Co): UTSA-16(Co), a cobalt citrate framework — Table 1 gives a pore size of 3.4-4.4 A, one step wider than the tightest; the rigid citrate linker also generates tight pores. Zeo++ classes it as ultramicroporous only, and finds no open metal sites.
