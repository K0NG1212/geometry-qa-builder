A bisporphyrin molecular tweezer carries two zinc porphyrins on a common spacer, and a guest with two nitrogen donors can coordinate one nitrogen to each zinc. How well that works depends on the spacer: the paper reports that the enediyne tweezer changes its Zn-Zn distance from 4.9 angstrom in the free host to 7.0 angstrom in the complex, that the stiff-stilbene tweezer moves only from 6.2 to 7.3 angstrom and can accommodate guests only by twisting its backbone, and that the glycoluril tweezer varies its interporphyrin distance through conformational changes of its seven-membered rings while porphyrin rotation stays limited. The fourth candidate is a single zinc porphyrin with one binding site. The guest here, 1,6-diaminohexane, is not rigid: its six-carbon chain can coil, so the distance between its two nitrogen atoms is not fixed and the guest can meet the host part of the way. Association constants were measured by UV-vis titration in dichloromethane at room temperature. You need to bind this flexible six-carbon diamine as tightly as the series allows, above four million per mole. The attached candidates.txt describes four candidate structures, all of them prepared and measured in Blom et al., Molecules (2015). The candidate must satisfy: (affinity) the measured association constant with 1,6-diaminohexane is at least 4 × 10^6 M^-1. Exactly one of the four does so in the measured data, which is not shown to you. Which candidate host should you choose?

Zn-TPP-single-porphyrin: Zn-TPP, a single zinc tetraphenylporphyrin with no spacer and no second porphyrin — One porphyrin, hence one zinc binding site: the guest can coordinate only at one end and none of the two-point cooperativity is available. It is the reference host in the paper.
tweezer-1-enediyne: the enediyne tweezer, whose spacer is a planar enediyne — The most flexible backbone of the three. Its Zn-Zn distance changes the most on binding, from 4.9 angstrom in the free tweezer to 7.0 angstrom in the complex, and the paper states that because its backbone is more flexible the cost of distortion on accommodating a guest is low.
tweezer-2-stiff-stilbene: the stiff-stilbene tweezer, whose spacer is a helical stiff stilbene — The least flexible spacer: its Zn-Zn distance moves only from 6.2 to 7.3 angstrom, and the paper states that it can accommodate guests only at the expense of distortion, primarily backbone twisting.
tweezer-3-glycoluril: the glycoluril tweezer, whose two porphyrins sit on a semi-rigid glycoluril spacer — Semi-rigid: it can vary its interporphyrin distance through conformational changes of the seven-membered rings, and its porphyrins cannot rotate freely, which makes it the more preorganised of the two adaptable tweezers.
