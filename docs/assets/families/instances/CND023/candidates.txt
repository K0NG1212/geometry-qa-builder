Zn-TPP-single-porphyrin: Zn-TPP, a single zinc tetraphenylporphyrin with no spacer and no second porphyrin — One porphyrin, hence one zinc binding site: the guest can coordinate only at one end and none of the two-point cooperativity is available. It is the reference host in the paper.
tweezer-1-enediyne: the enediyne tweezer, whose spacer is a planar enediyne — The most flexible backbone of the three. Its Zn-Zn distance changes the most on binding, from 4.9 angstrom in the free tweezer to 7.0 angstrom in the complex, and the paper states that because its backbone is more flexible the cost of distortion on accommodating a guest is low.
tweezer-2-stiff-stilbene: the stiff-stilbene tweezer, whose spacer is a helical stiff stilbene — The least flexible spacer: its Zn-Zn distance moves only from 6.2 to 7.3 angstrom, and the paper states that it can accommodate guests only at the expense of distortion, primarily backbone twisting.
tweezer-3-glycoluril: the glycoluril tweezer, whose two porphyrins sit on a semi-rigid glycoluril spacer — Semi-rigid: it can vary its interporphyrin distance through conformational changes of the seven-membered rings, and its porphyrins cannot rotate freely, which makes it the more preorganised of the two adaptable tweezers.
