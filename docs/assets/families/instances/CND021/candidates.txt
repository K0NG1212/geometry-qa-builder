PrS[5]Me: the methoxy-prism[5]arene, the same pentamer with methoxy chains — The same open pentagonal-prism cavity as the propoxy pentamer, with short methoxy groups instead of propoxy chains. The paper reports a bound tetramethylammonium guest well centred in this cavity, giving C-H...pi contacts with the walls.
PrS[5]nPr: the propoxy-prism[5]arene, a pentamer carrying propoxy chains — Five naphthalene units in an almost regular pentagonal prism, interior dihedral angles averaging the ideal 108 degrees. The cavity stays open, so a bound ammonium guest sits centred inside it making C-H...pi contacts with the walls; the chains here are the longest of the two pentamers.
PrS[6]Et: the ethoxy-prism[6]arene, the six-unit homologue with shorter ethoxy chains — The same cuboid hexamer skeleton, with ethoxy chains that pack inside more efficiently than propoxy: they fill 90 percent of the enclosed space and leave a free cavity volume of only 49 cubic angstroms, the smallest of the four.
PrS[6]nPr: the propoxy-prism[6]arene, the six-unit homologue with the same propoxy chains — Six naphthalene units in a cuboid. Four of its propoxy chains turn inward and pack against the aromatic walls, filling 84 percent of the enclosed space and leaving a free cavity volume of 77 cubic angstroms, the larger of the two hexamers.
