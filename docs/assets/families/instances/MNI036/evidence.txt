E1. With the same 3 % suspension, slides were lifted at 0.28 µm/s and at 1.66 µm/s; all other conditions (meniscus, evaporation, particles) are the same.
E2. Both deposits show their reflectance peak near 540 nm, close to the 547 nm expected for a close-packed FCC crystal of these spheres.
E3. At 1.66 µm/s the peak is broad (FWHM 167 nm) and weak (12 %); at 0.28 µm/s it is sharper (FWHM 66 nm) and stronger (66 %).
E4. Electron micrographs show a non-uniform monolayer at intermediate speed and low concentration, but a close-packed multilayer crystal at 0.28 µm/s.
