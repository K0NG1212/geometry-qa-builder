Scanning tunnelling spectra on the AA sites of twisted bilayer graphene at 1.10° and 1.15° are taken while a back gate changes the carrier density. The attached evidence.txt gives the evidence (facts E1–E4 from Kerelsky et al., Nature 572, 95 (2019); numbers as reported in the paper). Which interpretation of the 7.5 meV gap is consistent with all of these observations?

选项：
A. A single-particle band gap of the moiré bands, present whatever the filling.
B. The same gap that transport measures, with the same size, so transport fully captures it.

===== evidence.txt =====
E1. A gap of up to 7.5 meV (peak to peak) opens in the conduction vHs only when that peak reaches the Fermi level, which happens near half filling of the conduction moiré band (about 1.4 × 10^12 cm^-2); at other dopings the peak is not split.
E2. At charge neutrality both vHs peaks are present and unsplit.
E3. The gap is significantly larger than the activation energy of the resistance near the same filling in transport.
E4. Tip-induced band bending was seen in earlier STM work only at large twist angles, where the density of states is small; extrapolated to these small angles it is negligible.
