A zeolite membrane separates a liquid mixture by pervaporation: the feed contacts one side, the permeate is drawn off as vapour, and the zeolite framework's pore openings decide which molecule gets through. The task here is to pull methanol out of a 10 wt% methanol / methyl hexanoate mixture at 353 K; methanol is the small component, with a molecular size of 0.380 nm, while methyl hexanoate is a much larger ester. Four framework types were grown on the same porous alumina support tubes by the same secondary-growth method and tested under the same conditions. The paper gives pore diameters for three of them: CHA 0.38 nm and LTA 0.42 nm, both close to the size of methanol itself, and FAU 0.74 nm, which it calls the largest pore size among the membranes tested; it gives no pore diameter for the MFI framework. The separation factor below is the ratio that describes how much the permeate is enriched in methanol relative to the feed. You need a membrane that enriches methanol strongly in one pass, with a separation factor of at least two thousand. The attached candidates.txt describes four candidate structures, all of them prepared and measured in Ikeda et al., Membranes (2021). The candidate must satisfy: (enrichment) the measured methanol / methyl hexanoate separation factor at 353 K is at least 2000. Exactly one of the four does so in the measured data, which is not shown to you. Which candidate zeolite membrane should you choose?

===== candidates.txt =====
CHA: the CHA-type membrane — A pore diameter of 0.38 nm, essentially equal to the 0.380 nm size of methanol, so the aperture matches the molecule it is meant to pass as closely as any of the four.
FAU(10): the FAU-type membrane grown from a solution with a silica-to-alumina ratio of 10 — The FAU framework has a pore diameter of 0.74 nm, which the paper calls the largest among the membranes tested: roughly twice the 0.380 nm size of methanol, so methanol passes with little hindrance while the much larger ester does not.
LTA: the LTA-type membrane — A pore diameter of 0.42 nm, one step wider than the size of methanol and the second narrowest of the four.
MFI: the MFI-type membrane — The one framework for which the paper gives no pore diameter in the text; it was included because MFI membranes are known to separate alcohols from alcohol / ester mixtures.
