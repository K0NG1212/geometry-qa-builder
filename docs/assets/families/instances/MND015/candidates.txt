CHA: the CHA-type membrane — A pore diameter of 0.38 nm, essentially equal to the 0.380 nm size of methanol, so the aperture matches the molecule it is meant to pass as closely as any of the four.
FAU(10): the FAU-type membrane grown from a solution with a silica-to-alumina ratio of 10 — The FAU framework has a pore diameter of 0.74 nm, which the paper calls the largest among the membranes tested: roughly twice the 0.380 nm size of methanol, so methanol passes with little hindrance while the much larger ester does not.
LTA: the LTA-type membrane — A pore diameter of 0.42 nm, one step wider than the size of methanol and the second narrowest of the four.
MFI: the MFI-type membrane — The one framework for which the paper gives no pore diameter in the text; it was included because MFI membranes are known to separate alcohols from alcohol / ester mixtures.
