E1. Adding R5011 (0 to 4 wt%) raises the mobile-ion density from 1.7 to 2.9 × 10^13 cm^-3, and the dielectric loss at 1 kHz grows linearly with R5011 content.
E2. Adding S5011 (0 to 4 wt%) leaves the ion density at 1.6-1.9 × 10^13 cm^-3; the spectra overlap those of pure E44.
E3. 1:1 mixtures of R5011 and S5011 (no net chirality) at 1, 2 and 4 wt% in total give ion densities and diffusivities close to those of R5011-only samples at 0.5, 1 and 2 wt%.
E4. Antiparallel (6 µm) and 90°-twisted (5 µm) cells with the same alignment layers and electrodes give the same ionic behaviour.
E5. The R- and S-doped mixtures (2.62 and 2.57 wt%) have nearly the same helical pitch (0.355 and 0.362 µm) and nearly identical reflection bands.
