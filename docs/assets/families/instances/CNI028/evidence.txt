E1. In ONE the amide N (row 9) donates its H (row 11) to the ketone carbonyl O (row 8).
E2. In ELEVEN the amino N (row 3) donates its H (row 5) to the carbonyl O (row 13).
E3. Splitting between the antisymmetric and symmetric NH2 stretching frequencies (MP2/cc-pVTZ): ONE 159 cm^-1, ELEVEN 308 cm^-1; NH2 groups that take part in no hydrogen bond show about 120 cm^-1.
E4. At the H···O bond critical point the energy density H = G + V is positive (mainly electrostatic) for ONE and negative (partly covalent) for ELEVEN.
